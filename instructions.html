Instructions

Follow these steps to evaluate the audio samples:

  1. Click on the "Evaluate" tab to access the audio samples.
  2. Listen to each sample using the built-in audio player.
  3. Use the rating slider below each sample to rate it from 1 to 5.
  4. Your rating will be displayed next to the slider.
  5. Once you have rated all samples, your evaluations are complete.

Enjoy the experience and provide honest feedback!
